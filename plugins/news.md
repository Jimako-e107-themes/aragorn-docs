# News

Aragorn restyles the whole news plugin — listings, the article view, the
grid and every news menu — from Bootstrap 3 to Tabler markup. Nothing needs
configuring for this to take effect: the theme's template overrides are
picked up automatically once Aragorn is the active theme.

Where news appears in the theme:

| Page | Layout | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| News listing (`news.php`) | `sidebar` | Can also be the site's front page — see [news.php as front page](news-php.md). |
| Article view | `sidebar` | |
| News grid | any | A menu, usually placed on the home page — see [News grid](news-grid.md). |
| News menus (latest, category, archive…) | any | Sidebar or footer menu areas. |
